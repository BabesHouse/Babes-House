About Babes House

A place to make
something happen.

Babes House is a Tucson event space centered around queer community, connection, and having a genuinely good time.

We host intimate dinner parties, date nights, dance parties, and gatherings of all kinds. The idea is simple: make a space where people can meet, celebrate, eat, dance, flirt, create, and spend time together.

Whether you're here for a specific event or just looking for your next excuse to get out of the house, you're welcome at Babes House.
